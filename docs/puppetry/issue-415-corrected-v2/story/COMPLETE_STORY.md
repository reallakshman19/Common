# Narasimha Avatar - Complete four-scene story and dialogue

Grade 4 puppet show. The permanent palace stage does not change; timing target 180 seconds, untested.

## Scene 1: The Proud King (0:00-0:35, 35 seconds)

**0:00-0:08 - Narrator:** Long ago, there lived a powerful king named Hiranyakashipu. He was very proud.

**0:08-0:17 - King:** Ha! Ha! Ha! I am the greatest! Everyone must worship me!

**0:17-0:26 - Prahlada:** Father, Lord Vishnu is the greatest. He is everywhere!

**0:26-0:35 - King:** What? Vishnu? I will show you who is powerful!

Movement direction: Preset one King arm raised; raise only the controlled arm during boasting. Prahlada already has joined hands; do not command a new hand-folding joint. King shifts his pointing arm toward the same closed pillar to bridge Scene 2.

## Scene 2: The Magical Pillar (0:35-1:15, 40 seconds)

**0:35-0:43 - King:** Prahlada! You say Lord Vishnu is everywhere?

**0:43-0:50 - Prahlada:** Yes, Father! Lord Vishnu is everywhere!

**0:50-0:59 - King / Prahlada:** King: Is he inside this pillar too? Prahlada: Yes, Father!

**0:59-1:08 - King:** Then let him come out!

**1:08-1:15 - Narrator:** Suddenly, the pillar began to shake! A mighty roar filled the palace!

Movement direction: Move King closer and point active arm toward the pillar. Three small tap gestures coordinated with KNOCK, KNOCK, BOOM; forceful contact is not required. Decorative tremble only; doors stay closed until 1:15.

## Scene 3: The Arrival of Narasimha (1:15-2:10, 55 seconds)

**1:15-1:25 - Narrator:** BOOM! The pillar broke open! Lord Vishnu appeared as Narasimha - half man and half lion!

**1:25-1:35 - Narasimha:** ROAAAR! I have come to protect my dear devotee Prahlada!

**1:35-1:45 - King:** Who are you? I cannot be defeated by a man or an animal!

**1:45-1:57 - Narasimha:** I am neither a man nor an animal! I am Narasimha!

**1:57-2:10 - Narrator:** At twilight, Lord Narasimha defeated the cruel king and saved Prahlada. Goodness won over evil!

Movement direction: Open both rearward-swinging doors and hold them open. Narasimha moves through the opening with blessing arm DOWN; rise only when clear. Roar by voice/body motion; King recoils and exits left; no violence contact.

## Scene 4: Goodness Wins! (2:10-3:00, 50 seconds)

**2:10-2:20 - Prahlada:** Thank you, Lord Narasimha! You saved me!

**2:20-2:32 - Narasimha:** Dear Prahlada, your faith and kindness make you strong. Always follow the path of truth!

**2:32-2:42 - Prahlada:** Thank you, Lord! I will always be kind, brave and truthful!

**2:42-2:53 - Narrator:** And so, Lord Narasimha protected Prahlada. This story teaches us that goodness always wins over evil!

**2:53-3:00 - Everyone:** Be brave! Be kind! Do what is right! Jai Narasimha!

Movement direction: Once Narasimha is fully in front of the opening, raise his one controlled arm to bless. Prahlada bows using whole-body tilt of about 15 degrees, then returns upright. Complete group chant and final small bow without another backdrop or mechanical curtain.
