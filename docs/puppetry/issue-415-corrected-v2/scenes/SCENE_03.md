# Narasimha Avatar — Scene 3 (candidate)

## Scene 3: The Arrival of Narasimha (1:15-2:10, 55 seconds)

**1:15-1:25 - Narrator:** BOOM! The pillar broke open! Lord Vishnu appeared as Narasimha - half man and half lion!

**1:25-1:35 - Narasimha:** ROAAAR! I have come to protect my dear devotee Prahlada!

**1:35-1:45 - King:** Who are you? I cannot be defeated by a man or an animal!

**1:45-1:57 - Narasimha:** I am neither a man nor an animal! I am Narasimha!

**1:57-2:10 - Narrator:** At twilight, Lord Narasimha defeated the cruel king and saved Prahlada. Goodness won over evil!

Movement direction: Open both rearward-swinging doors and hold them open. Narasimha moves through the opening with blessing arm DOWN; rise only when clear. Roar by voice/body motion; King recoils and exits left; no violence contact.
