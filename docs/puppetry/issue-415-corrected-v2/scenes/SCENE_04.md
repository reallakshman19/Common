# Narasimha Avatar — Scene 4 (candidate)

## Scene 4: Goodness Wins! (2:10-3:00, 50 seconds)

**2:10-2:20 - Prahlada:** Thank you, Lord Narasimha! You saved me!

**2:20-2:32 - Narasimha:** Dear Prahlada, your faith and kindness make you strong. Always follow the path of truth!

**2:32-2:42 - Prahlada:** Thank you, Lord! I will always be kind, brave and truthful!

**2:42-2:53 - Narrator:** And so, Lord Narasimha protected Prahlada. This story teaches us that goodness always wins over evil!

**2:53-3:00 - Everyone:** Be brave! Be kind! Do what is right! Jai Narasimha!

Movement direction: Once Narasimha is fully in front of the opening, raise his one controlled arm to bless. Prahlada bows using whole-body tilt of about 15 degrees, then returns upright. Complete group chant and final small bow without another backdrop or mechanical curtain.
