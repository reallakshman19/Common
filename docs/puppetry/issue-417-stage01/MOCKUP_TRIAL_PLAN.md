## Engineering change after first screen

The Stage-01 geometric study used **two 70 × 240 mm flaps in an exactly 140 × 240 mm opening**. That is a zero-play candidate and is inappropriate to assume will work when cut from thick cardboard. The disposable trial therefore uses:

| Trial part | Dimensions, mm | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed pillar blank | 180 wide × 280 high | Original stage candidate |
| Opening | x=20..160, y=15..255 -> 140 × 240 | 20-mm posts, 15-mm sill, 25-mm lintel |
| Two leaf cutouts | **69 × 237 each** | Creates 2-mm nominal centre gap and 1.5-mm top/bottom play |
| Door hinge axes | x=20/160 at proposed plane z=40 | Vertical axes, rearward 90° swing |
| Narasimha arm-down rectangular gauge | 92 × 180 | Conservative first passage fixture, NOT silhouette |
| Narasimha blessing envelope | 155 × 180 | MUST deploy only after front clearance |
| Pillar printable template | two A4 sheets world y=0..145 and 135..280 | 10-mm registration overlap at world y=140 |
| Calibration lines | 100 mm horizontal on every page; 50 mm vertical where unobstructed | Check actual printer output |

All values are **mock-up hypotheses**, not an Owner-approved final design. No physical trial has been observed.

## Practical material list (trial only)

- Cardboard blank at least 200 × 300 mm for the continuous pillar frame, or joined/reinforced blank if needed.
- Two additional strips at least 70 × 230 mm each for the door leaves; reserve spare material.
- A4 ordinary printer paper (seven pages for generator output), paper tape, a ruler, pencil and scissors.
- Low-force flexible cloth/tape hinges, removable soft latch tabs; light string for optional one-pull linkage experiment.
- Simple removable frame supports and a stage backdrop with 750 × 400 mm proposed visible area.
- Rounded cardboard silhouette placeholder and **actual** Narasimha source cutout/rods for decisive M1-M3.
- Operator cue/timer and paper evidence sheet. **Adult performs cutting and hole-punching; no exposed pins, sharp wires or strong springs.**

## Critical three-dimensional assembly condition

**Do not mount the pillar flush to a solid palace backdrop or over a solid tabletop:** the frame is provisionally at z=40 mm, the unchanged permanent rear palace wall at z=150 mm, and Narasimha must pass z=130 to z=20. Keep the central corridor x=305..445, z=15..140 and at least 140 mm beneath baseline open for the actual main stick and arm control. Proposed removable side returns occupy only x=285..300 and x=450..465 at z=40..150; they require separate adult stability, tape-hinge and oblique-sightline tests. See [PORTAL_AND_ROD_ASSEMBLY_CANDIDATE.md](PORTAL_AND_ROD_ASSEMBLY_CANDIDATE.md). These numbers are hypotheses, not source-verified cardboard measurements.

## Build and comparison sequence

1. Print generated **100% actual-size A4 vector PDF** without scaling; measure 100-mm and 50-mm calibration bars. Printer is NOT validated merely by PDF vector geometry.
2. Join the two pillar **paper** tiles on common y=140 registration crosses; use traced full outline to transfer the whole 180 × 280 mm frame onto larger card. **Do not cut the frame as two separate 145-mm cardboard halves without reinforcement.**
3. Adult cuts aperture x20..160, y15..245. Cut two 69 × 237 mm leaves, holes Ø2 placed 10 mm from free edge at midheight; reinforce thread holes.
4. Tape each leaf at its **OUTER** vertical edge so it swings backward. With the two leaves shut, measure the real seam and vertical gaps; if excessive light reveals Narasimha, fix *masking* without creating a jam.
5. **Experiment B baseline:** helper manually opens both leaves rearward to about 90°, parks them with simple removable soft tabs, then operator lifts/moves Narasimha arm-DOWN through the opening. Verify actual puppet silhouette and rod travel.
6. **Experiment A challenge:** try two light strings joined into one pull. Determine actual routing, pull force, stops and whether both leaves open and remain open unattended. **Do not declare a functioning one-pull linkage until it is demonstrated.**
7. Compare B and A against observed hand count, timing, reliability and pinch risks; park sliding doors unless necessary.
8. Physically verify an **OPEN x305..445, z15..140 shaft corridor**, at least **140 mm unobstructed BELOW y=0**, and a **110-mm fore/aft movement** from z130 to z20. A continuous tabletop under this route fails regardless of a correct door opening.
9. Test audience concealment before reveal; the new 2-mm centre seam is a specific potential light leak.
10. Record M1-M8 from the PDF test sheet and photos/measurement notes. The M9 full 180-second show remains a separate qualification gate.

## Critical operator choreography: 1:08–1:25

- 1:08–1:15: three knock sounds; only decorative pillar tremble; **no unstable stage shaking**.
- 1:15: ensure Narasimha's blessing arm is stowed and door strings are not in the puppet path.
- By 1:25: both leaves open and parked; puppet has moved from hidden plane toward front and risen within the 230-mm clear opening.
- During whole passage, require real gap >=10 mm including control rods.
- Later, after the puppet is fully clear, extend blessing arm; never extend 155-mm width in 140-mm aperture.
