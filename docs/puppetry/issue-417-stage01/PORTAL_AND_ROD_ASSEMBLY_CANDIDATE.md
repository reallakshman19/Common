# STAGE-01-MOCKUP — buildable passage topology and operator handoff (CANDIDATE)

**Parent:** [#415](https://github.com/reallakshman19/Common/issues/415)  
**Investigation:** [#417](https://github.com/reallakshman19/Common/issues/417)  
**Draft study:** [PR #418](https://github.com/reallakshman19/Common/pull/418)

**Finding before any physical build:** The earlier seven-page paper kit sizes the front pillar and two doors but does not sufficiently show *where that door frame goes in depth*, what physically holds it in front of the palace backdrop, and where the puppet's long rear control stick travels. A frame glued flush to a solid palace backdrop, or a continuous tabletop across its control corridor, defeats the reveal despite passing a 2D width screen. This note amends the **assembly hypothesis**, not the printed cut outlines or original puppet assets.

## 1. One permanent stage — proposed x/y/z relationships

Keep one backdrop throughout all four scenes. This trial treats it as a **rear wall at z=150 mm**, not an obstacle in the opening plane. The fixed pillar-door plane is **z=40 mm**, i.e. 110 mm *in front* of that wall. Narasimha waits at **z=130 mm** behind closed leaves and advances to **z=20 mm** after opening. All distances are candidate millimetres, with z=0 nearest the audience.

## 2. Proposed rear support and concealment

- On the **outside faces** of the left and right pillar posts, trial two light cardboard side returns from z40 to z150: left x285..300, right x450..465. They form opaque side wings and connect the front frame to the rear backdrop, **without occupying aperture x305..445**. These are *optional trial braces*, not yet tested for stability.
- The 5 mm separation from each nominal hinge axis must be measured against real hinge/tape thickness. If the returns collide with opened doors or are unstable, replace them with external stage clamps/supports; do not move them inside the passage.
- Mask Narasimha from side audience positions; check sightlines at x=0 and x=750, not just straight on.
- A separate removable soft door stop or helper must hold leaves open. Door side returns are **not** proof that the doors latch themselves.

## 3. OPEN bottom: no tabletop through puppet controls

```text
SIDE VIEW — z front (0) to rear (150)

   front N z20       doors z40             hidden N z130    backdrop z150
          |              |                       |                |
y > 0    [N]             |                       [N]              |rear
----------+--------------+-----------------------+----------------+   y=0 nominal baseline
          |                              | 
          |<----- 110 mm fore-aft ------>|
          |       rod travels           |
          |  OPEN HAND/ROD PATH         |
          v  >=140 mm BELOW y=0         v

A solid floor/tabletop in the central corridor would trap the stick.
```

**Trial floor exclusion zone:** keep x305..445 and z15..140 unobstructed for the moving shaft and its connectors, plus at least **140 mm vertical clearance below y=0**. This is a **deliberate design provision**, not a measured result. Adult-secured side supports may sit outside this corridor. If a theatre necessarily has a floor or table, it must have a safely protected equivalent slot that never catches hands/rods; do not cut a live tabletop in an improvised trial.

The analysis only locates the proposed main stick path. The 220 mm Narasimha arm-control rod and coupler are not geometrically proven and must be included in the real clearance experiment. No rod is allowed to scrape or pinch against sharp slot edges.

## 4. Build experiment and stop conditions

1. Print existing Stage-01 PDF on ordinary A4 at 100%, measure both calibration scales.
2. Adult transfer paper tiles to a *continuous or reinforced* 180×280 mm frame. Cut the 140×230 aperture. Cut two **69×227** trial doors and hinge on the outer edges so they swing rearward.
3. **Do not fix the frame flush against the palace rear wall.** Mount at z40 with a removable, adequately supported 110-mm stand-off and side masking outside the passage. Verify it cannot fall when doors open.
4. Keep the central floor/handle corridor clear; verify sufficient controlled fore/aft movement between z130 and z20 and below-baseline space.
5. Use the original Narasimha *candidate* cutting guide (separate PDF) and an actual rod/coupler mock-up. The 92×180 rectangular gauge is a preliminary fit screen only.
6. Test **manual** door opening and positive hold-open first. Record required hands and five full reveal cycles.
7. Only then experiment with one-pull strings, routing, stop and latch. Reject if it requires extra hands or catches the puppet.
8. Record measured minimum left/right, top/bottom and rod clearance throughout travel, door stability, audience visibility and real 1:15–1:25 timing.

**STOP / do not claim PASS** if the real stick route intersects a solid surface, backdrop placement blocks the puppet, door/standoff returns intrude into the clear opening, the puppet is visible before reveal, the opening requires an undeclared helping hand, or the stand cannot be kept stable safely.
