## Invariants

- One static palace backdrop for the complete play.
- Three puppets: King Hiranyakashipu, Prahlada and Narasimha; one centre-opening pillar.
- Scene timing: 35 + 40 + 55 + 50 = **180 seconds**.
- No curtains/background changes between scenes.
- Narasimha concealed behind pillar before reveal; King removed after defeat.
- Stage violence should be suggested by movement and narration only, not shown graphically.

## Scene 1 — The Proud King (0:00–0:35)

| Clock | Speaker | Exact candidate line / operator cue |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Narrator | Long ago, there lived a powerful king named Hiranyakashipu. He was very proud. |
| 0:08–0:17 | King | Ha! Ha! Ha! I am the greatest! Everyone must worship me! |
| 0:17–0:26 | Prahlada | Father, Lord Vishnu is the greatest. He is everywhere! |
| 0:26–0:35 | King | What? Vishnu? I will show you who is powerful! |

**Operator actions:** King raises both arms while boasting; Prahlada's hands remain folded and he speaks calmly. King turns toward the fixed centre pillar at the scene boundary. The pillar remains closed.

## Scene 2 — The Magical Pillar (0:35–1:15)

| Clock | Speaker | Exact candidate line / operator cue |
|---|---|---|
| 0:35–0:43 | King | Prahlada! You say Lord Vishnu is everywhere? |
| 0:43–0:50 | Prahlada | Yes, Father! Lord Vishnu is everywhere! |
| 0:50–0:59 | King, then Prahlada | **King:** Is he inside this pillar too? **Prahlada:** Yes, Father! |
| 0:59–1:08 | King | Then let him come out! |
| 1:08–1:15 | Narrator | Suddenly, the pillar began to shake! A mighty roar filled the palace! |

**Operator actions:** King approaches and points toward the pillar, strikes it three times (KNOCK! KNOCK! BOOM!). The pillar shakes gently; the flap opening begins only at the Scene 3 reveal. Prahlada remains calm with folded hands.

## Scene 3 — The Arrival of Lord Narasimha (1:15–2:10)

| Clock | Speaker | Exact candidate line / operator cue |
|---|---|---|
| 1:15–1:25 | Narrator | BOOM! The pillar broke open! Lord Vishnu appeared as Narasimha — half man and half lion! |
| 1:25–1:35 | Narasimha | ROAAAR! I have come to protect my dear devotee Prahlada! |
| 1:35–1:45 | King | Who are you? I cannot be defeated by a man or an animal! |
| 1:45–1:57 | Narasimha | I am neither a man nor an animal! I am Narasimha! |
| 1:57–2:10 | Narrator | At twilight, Lord Narasimha defeated the cruel king and saved Prahlada. Goodness won over evil! |

**Operator actions:** Open the two pillar flaps and raise Narasimha from its hidden position; the King recoils backward and is lowered off-stage. Prahlada stays in prayer. No violent contact or change of background.

## Scene 4 — Goodness Wins (2:10–3:00)

| Clock | Speaker | Exact candidate line / operator cue |
|---|---|---|
| 2:10–2:20 | Prahlada | Thank you, Lord Narasimha! You saved me! |
| 2:20–2:32 | Narasimha | Dear Prahlada, your faith and kindness make you strong. Always follow the path of truth! |
| 2:32–2:42 | Prahlada | Thank you, Lord! I will always be kind, brave and truthful! |
| 2:42–2:53 | Narrator | And so, Lord Narasimha protected Prahlada. This story teaches us that goodness always wins over evil! |
| 2:53–3:00 | Everyone | Be brave! Be kind! Do what is right! Jai Narasimha! |

**Operator actions:** Prahlada bows with folded hands; Narasimha raises a blessing arm; Prahlada returns upright; both bow, end play.
