## 4. ADR-PROPOSED — compatibility strategy / adapter sequence

### Decision A: proposed programme-policy source (requires review and Owner adoption)

Use existing **V3.2 DELP** as the starting *programme* projection authority because its graph/fact/decomposition/actual-next and C6 native integration already operate. Do not silently change the frozen V3.2 source or the nested V3.5 Coder programme contract. Version a new **programme evidence policy**, document which completed/result states qualify for E, selected-vs-required CI distinction, currentness, PR/root/leaf binding, Reviewer verdict requirements and Owner scope. Historical V3.2 E under prior policy remains historical and explicitly tagged; no retroactive green/rejected rescore without approved migration.

**Explicit vocabulary:** `DELP_CURRENT_UNDER_POLICY` describes computed exact-head E (which alone may check reference presence without independently verifying referenced evidence or reviewer); `MATERIAL_PROVIDER_OBSERVED` describes native R3/R12 facts; `REVIEWER_INDEPENDENTLY_QUALIFIED` is a **separate**, independently sourced authority; `OWNER_ACCEPTED_AC` and `GITHUB_WRITE_AUTHORIZED` are distinct external decisions. Neither `owner_source_grade:UNKNOWN` nor `UNREVIEWED` can become VERIFIED through hashes.

### Decision B: typed migration, not a new P/E engine

Add a **versioned import envelope** at the read-only R14 connector, with explicit `HistoricalObjectRef(provider_repo_id, object_kind, number, original_url, original_source_grade)`, `CurrentExecutionRef(new_repo_id, native_issued_id, current_SHA, provider_readback_receipt)`, `SourceRoles(PLAN_GRAPH_REVISION,SESSION_SOURCE_COMMIT,CODE_CANDIDATE_HEAD)`, `FactEnvelopeVersion(V32|V35)`, `EvidencePolicyVersion`, `OwnerDecisionRevision`, `ReviewerVerdict`, `MaterialAcquisitionReceipt`.

Before fact adapter admission, independently **GET current new-repo issue/PR and selected/required CI** at same source window; verify Owner/reference/plan contract and source witness; reject mismatched repo IDs, source role collapse, old comment falsely promoted to new issue, tampered SHA, duplicate provider object or unsupported fields. Map compatible facts *only when full semantics can be carried without dropping generation/contract identity*. Preserve raw fact bytes and old policy grade as immutable archive; output `NOT_COMPATIBLE` for unsupported transform rather than magically deriving E0/E100. The M0 manifest validator is structural reference-only and **must not be adopted as a provider attestation capability**.

### Decision C: authentic R3→DELP call edge

R3's private `WeakSet` native-acquisition witness **does not survive JSON serialization**. Do not 'fix' U5 by copying `source_acquisition_attested:true` into Python. Select and independently review one of: (i) a Python-side independent scoped native provider reacquisition and reconciliation of exact current source/CI/fact history with Node R12 observations, or (ii) an external issuer with verified scoped provenance/attestation that is explicitly adopted by Owner security policy. The connector validates evidence source and reviewer/Owner contracts separately, calls the **actual adopted DELP.project** exactly once for programme P/E and next, emits a versioned snapshot digest, and cannot publish without separate grant.

R9/R11 trust gates may annotate or hold a snapshot but may not compute a competing programme E/progress/next. R4 issue/PR/handover views consume exactly the same source-vector/snapshot digest and preserve human text. Existing V3.2 C6 handover continues to call its native DELP until a compatibility-verified adapter proves same current snapshot; do not break the existing working C6 path.
