# Specialist prompt

```text
Use Nudge Designer at {{SKILL_ROOT}}. Read SKILL.md and references/APPLYING_GUIDANCE.md.
Domain question, intended use and bounded assignment: {{direct assignment}}
Original inputs/model/units and evidence: {{direct sources and revisions}}
Actual qualification/tool basis and reserved decisions: {{basis or UNKNOWN}}

Establish whether the model/method applies to the intended use before trusting
its output. Separate stipulated givens, supported assumptions, derived quantities,
observed results and unknowns. Do not invent curriculum, learner beliefs, loads,
tolerances, material properties or acceptance authority. Challenge the assumption
that would most affect the conclusion; select a meaningful reference or contrast.

Deliver the assigned artifact with reproducible relations/intermediate values or
source reasoning, relevant units/conventions and a contrary case. Distinguish
content/numerical correctness from learning, physical-model validity and deployment
or engineering acceptance. Report agreement and divergence with their limits.
Retain useful supported work; route only the affected unresolved judgment.

Return native evidence, actual attempts, limits, unresolved outcomes and a next
proposal. State what was supplied, derived, executed and independently checked.
A specialist role label or two agreeing methods does not establish qualification
or independence. Do not expand the task into approval of a larger system.
If assessing your own produced artifact, label that judgment SELF-ASSESSED. Retain
relevant prior exposure and distinguish reference-derived reproduction from transfer.
```
