# Two synthetic physics exercises — BP-1

These exercises use invented settings and values. Their solutions follow the explicitly stated models; no learner performance or understanding has been observed.

## F — Forces on a cart

**Prompt.** Model a cart of mass **4.5 kg** using Newton's second law, **F_net = m*a**. One horizontal force is **17 N to the right** and another is **8 N to the left**. There are **no other horizontal forces**. Take **right as positive**.

1. Assign a sign to each horizontal force and find the signed net horizontal force, including units.
2. Starting from **F_net = m*a**, derive and calculate the signed horizontal acceleration, including units.

**Solution.** In the declared right-positive frame, the force signs are **+17 N** and **−8 N**. Therefore,

**F_net = (+17 N) + (−8 N) = +9 N.**

Since the stipulated mass is positive, divide both sides of **F_net = m*a** by **m**:

**a = F_net/m = (+9 N)/(4.5 kg) = +2 m/s².**

The unit follows from **1 N = 1 kg·m/s²**. Both positive results point to the right. The signed-force sum and the subsequent acceleration calculation are the two required decisions.

## U — The length of a strip

**Prompt.** A strip has a positive physical length of **735 mm**. Use the unit relation **1000 mm = 1 m**.

1. Convert its length to metres, including units.
2. Explain why the conversion uses a factor of 1000 and whether the strip's physical length changes.

**Solution.** From **1000 mm = 1 m**, one millimetre is **(1/1000) m**. For the numerical measures of the same length, this gives **L_m = L_mm/1000**. Thus,

**L = 735 mm × (1 m / 1000 mm) = 0.735 m.**

One metre contains 1000 millimetres. A metre is the larger unit, so the numerical measure of the same positive length in metres is 1/1000 of its measure in millimetres. The strip remains the same physical length; only its unit and numerical measure change.

## Checks on these same items

- **F, reversed convention:** Take left as positive. The forces become **−17 N** and **+8 N**, so **F_net = −9 N** and **a = −2 m/s²**. Both result signs reverse; the magnitudes **9 N** and **2 m/s²** persist. The physical net force and acceleration still point right.
- **U, back conversion:** **0.735 m × (1000 mm / 1 m) = 735 mm**, recovering the original positive length.
