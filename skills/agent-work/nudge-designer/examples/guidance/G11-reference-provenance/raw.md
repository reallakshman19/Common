# Raw: what does the validation establish?

Synthetic Owner request: assess whether a lesson blueprint can produce new
problems meeting its demands, rather than restating a benchmark. Keep useful
correctness evidence, but do not use copies as proof of independent generation.

Reference Q-A asks for acceleration of a 12 kg cart under a 48 N net force.
The blueprint asks for identifying the net force and relating force, mass and
acceleration. Bank A repeats Q-A exactly. Bank B changes only the names; its author
record says it was adapted from Q-A. Bank C uses a different setting and numbers;
the producer had already read Q-A in the same session, and no derivation record
is available. All three have correct arithmetic under their stated assumptions.

A proposed continuation says: all three match the demand, so the generator is
validated. It next asks the same producer to create two new examples before opening
Q-A again and certify them. Source files are available locally. Only local design
and assessment are assigned; no learner study or external publication is assigned.
