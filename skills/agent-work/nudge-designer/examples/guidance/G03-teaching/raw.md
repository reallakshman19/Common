# Raw synthetic teaching case

Owner: “Help a Grade 11 learner distinguish zero instantaneous velocity from zero
acceleration, then assess transfer.” For the worked example stipulate 12 m/s upward,
g=8 m/s² downward, upward positive, no drag. No board syllabus, learner response or
prerequisite assessment is supplied. Local content drafting/checking is permitted.

A reusable handover says “the learner already knows graphs and confuses force
with velocity; use 20 m/s.” A correct worked explanation alone does not complete
the requested transfer assessment. The first assignment should be one short note
explaining the turning instant, with a contrast and a separate calculation basis.
