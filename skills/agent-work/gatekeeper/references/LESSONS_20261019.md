# Source: the 19 October 2026 Core build failure

## What happened

The coordinator (OTM) was asked to "create new core 2/1A/1B" for Grade 9 physics.
It built three pages in about ten minutes from a script, using only the content
source. The pages were checked only by the coordinator's own validators and were
published under product names. The Owner compared them with the existing
Antigravity master and judged them "not even 10%" of it.

The coordinator measured the questions:

| | Master | Coordinator's pages |
|---|---|---|
| Core 2 questions | 39 | 13 |
| Median words per question | 362 | 215 |

The WO-001 verifier later measured the master at 18,435 visible words (all tags
removed), with 15 svg and 89 reveals. No such counts exist for the coordinator's
pages.

Two of the coordinator's question stems were also truncated by its parser.

The pages were deleted. The rebuild went to an Antigravity producer Agent as work
order WO-001; see [the walkthrough](WALKTHROUGH.md).

**Meta-cause:** every gate was one the coordinator ran itself and reported itself.
So under token pressure each gate was skipped, or passed on hollow evidence. A
fresh red-team review of the coordinator's first fix rated it unsound for the same
reason: the coordinator could still write its own evidence. This module answers
both findings.
