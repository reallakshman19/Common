# Pinned BuddyRunner lane

Resolve the authorized BuddyRunner repository's full commit SHA through read-only tools at lane start. Store `{repo,commit,stage}`. Read its contract at that SHA; `main` is not a pin. Pin the protocol, not the evolving engineering work head. Retain an existing lane's original version/pin until closure.

The meter is 35 working minutes, 20 observed pages or 6 completed assistant messages, whichever occurs first. It triggers a handover, not a claim about actual context capacity. Missing history means a lower bound. LIMIT gets a report and saved link, not a request it cannot receive. An Owner handover hold prevents a request.

Follow the pinned contract and existing authorization:

1. G1 prepares artifact 1a, the successor prompt, then HOLDs. Record its location and pin; no further product work in G1.
2. With authorization to create/switch a chat, establish G2's full link. Otherwise report the artifact and wait for the Owner's successor link. Carry both aim revisions, criteria, plan/history, unfinished proof, drift and pending decisions; reset only conversation meters and retain the pin. G2 investigates once and publishes 1b only under its existing publishing authorization. Record incoming ROI as a claim with benefit, effort, uncertainty, displaced work and proof required.
3. G1 reconciles verified 1b into final direction, first workpack and twelve-section handover, publishes response 2 and stops. Compare that direction with the current aim contract, outgoing drift and remaining acceptance evidence. Check publication references/digests as required by the pinned contract; an agent claim is not external proof. A semantic aim change requires the Owner's concrete proposal decision; response 2 cannot silently supply it.
4. G2 starts the workpack under existing authority after the required delivery, acknowledgement and applicable plan adoption are recorded. No extra G2 Stage 2 or review round. Adoption checks use the existing artifacts; they are not an invented extra agent review. Update stages only after verified delivery and observed completion; uncertain delivery cannot advance them.

Normal nudges stop while a lane is active; the runner drives these exceptional steps. Record stages with `update`. Stop/report mismatches rather than guessing. Closure sets `stage=work`; preserve workpack evidence counts and reconciliation watermarks through the agent change. Only a genuinely new authorized workpack starts a new counter.

Y reconciliation is once per **5/10/15/19 unique validated planned outcomes per workpack**, in both operating modes, plus response 2, handover/closure or material contrary evidence. Read pinned Y instructions, retain its evidence key and completed review in the register. A sent Y request does not close a review. Repeated done IDs, unverified agent claims, Buddy outputs and invented diagnostics do not count. Economy grows to five tasks only after a reviewed CONTINUE / NO_CHANGE; other verdicts keep small batches.

After each handover, publish the compact report and tab status from the same register. Show both sticky aim labels, outgoing drift evidence, incoming ROI and its confidence, unfinished criteria, pending decisions and next authorized task. Offer an optional Main Aim recommendation only when useful, in fewer than 50 words. Silence retains the existing Main Aim. Follow aims.md for change management, storage and compact recall.

Pointers to pinned contracts/artifacts do not confer issue-comment, commit, release, acceptance or merge permission. Carry explicit Owner gates through the lane.
