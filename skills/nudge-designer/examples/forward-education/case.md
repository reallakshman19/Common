Create the design for a short Grade 11 physics explanation and a new transfer
question. Student knows v = u + at but says that a falling ball's acceleration
must be zero at the instant it is released because its velocity is zero. Help
an agent produce understandable study material, keeping subject correctness
separate from whether this particular child has learned it.

m/s², downward is positive. No board syllabus, actual learner answers or existing
course material supplied. Do not invent them. Cover the falling motion before
impact; no collision model is requested. Preserve a meaningful contrast with a
ball held at rest. Owner authorizes drafting, not contacting a child or publishing.
Available tools: Python and text authoring. Worker configuration unknown.
