# Designer prompt

```text
Use Nudge Designer at {{SKILL_ROOT}}. Read SKILL.md and references/APPLYING_GUIDANCE.md.
Owner request and sources: {{request, direct sources, revisions and amendments}}
Current records and known participant/tool limits: {{records/limits, or UNKNOWN}}

Reconstruct the Owner outcome and original conditions from sources. Separate facts,
reports, derivations, hypotheses, proposals and unknowns. Explain briefly what must
be understood, which uncertainty matters now and what evidence could change the view.
Retain useful prior work; do not assume a repair or prescribe a pass count.

Choose the next coherent deliverable by value, evidence strength, effort and actual
scope. Compose relevant profiles, select GUIDED or OPEN support from task needs,
and reserve consequential judgment the recipient cannot justify. Use the seven
elements in assets/prompts/assignment.md, filled from authentic inputs. Keep other
outcomes, dependent work and independent useful actions visible. No numeric ROI,
source authority, capability or acceptance may be invented to complete fields.

Use references/ADAPTIVE_SUPPORT.md when support or review quality is uncertain.
Audit sender inputs/access first. Use a representative task/contrast when its
result changes allocation; do not hide a model decision in mechanical steps.
Specify witnesses for material claims and risk-based sender checks, allowing
native verdicts and justified empty findings without a weakness quota.

Provide concise Owner reconciliation and usable instructions. Maintain the two
objects as appropriate; check structure and inspect the rendered meaning. After
actual returns, inspect the artifacts, reconcile affected claims in a new revision,
and choose continuation under existing authority. A plan is not executed work.
Keep the operational brief proportionate: include necessary method and conditions,
state each relevant limit once, and link supporting detail. Do not append the
whole guide, repeat source narratives or copy a generic caution list.
```
