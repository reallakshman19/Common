# Optional Owner comprehension nudge (BuddyRunner v1.7)

This is **not a phase, a required acknowledgement, an additional review of response 2, or a permission gate**. After the ordinary handover is closed, an Owner may ask these questions *during normal successor work* if a material misunderstanding needs correction. Do not duplicate a completed 1b/2, invent approval, or launch new agents.

> From actual authoritative issue and current source, answer in your own words:
>
> **Role and predecessor:** Who are you? Who owned this work earlier? What has the predecessor truly delivered, and what belongs to you versus other agents/reviewers/controllers?
>
> **Vertical scope:** Who owns adjacent architecture, authentic inputs, delegated/specialist tasks, verification and release? How do those responsibilities map to concrete modules, data and consumers?
>
> **Artifact authority:** What Owner requirements, schemas/blueprints, authentic/private/derived test inputs, golden fixtures and benchmark commands are authoritative? For each, what is merely a CANDIDATE, what is designated, what was approved by whom and *for what scope*, what was really executed, and what is UNKNOWN? Cite actual version, SHA, receipt or decision. No inference from labels or passing counts.
>
> **Original-issue gap:** What was the user originally meant to do, what can the current application actually do, and what remains broken/unproven/awaiting approval? State first observed failure and separate cause from hypothesis.
>
> **Top ROI and first task:** Propose up to three bounded changes or NO_CHANGE, with user benefit, local alternative, assigned owner, evidence, cost/risk, a falsifiable acceptance observation and stop/rollback. Name what you need before coding.
>
> Mark VERIFIED/REPORTED/NOT_RUN/UNKNOWN explicitly. Do not change code, grant yourself authority, or ask G1 for another Stage 2 by answering this nudge.

If 1b/2 already established these points, **do not require a repeat answer**.
