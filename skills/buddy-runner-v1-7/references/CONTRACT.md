# Shared v1.7 grounding and closure contract

## Governing question

**What was the Owner trying to make possible, what does current source and evidence establish, and what is the smallest justified next step toward that outcome?**

Trace current issue → parent → roadmap and amendments → original inputs → exact source → real consumer/output. Treat prior reports and case-packet hints as hypotheses. Distinguish verified Owner instruction, mirrored claim, agent interpretation and UNKNOWN. Preserve the original outcome even when a predecessor implementation is wrong.

## Engineering judgment

- Trace the end-to-end user journey through named functions and source paths. Inspect competing implementations and the runtime/deployment entrypoint where relevant.
- Find the earliest demonstrated contract failure, or state the earliest boundary beyond which success is unproven. An evidence gap is not proof of data loss or root cause.
- Separate established cause, plausible hypothesis and unknown. Name the smallest discriminating benchmark or observation; do not start a broad test campaign during investigation.
- Challenge the route: can a mistaken assumption, unnecessary dependency, ordering or ownership split be removed? Compare a local fix with a bounded alternative using existing working modules. Select at most three high-ROI changes; park nonessential redesign.
- Give each responsibility one proposed module owner. Resolve overlapping source identities, scopes, revision/cancellation/storage semantics, consent/exports and deployment seams when applicable. Do not combine incompatible implementations by saying "merge both".
- Make a decision with consequences: KEEP/NO_CHANGE, CORRECT, VERIFY_FIRST or BLOCKED. VERIFY_FIRST must name the oracle and next action for each result. BLOCKED must name the precise decision/input and unblock condition. Do not invent busywork.

Complete this sentence for a substantive recommendation: **Because [specific source/evidence] establishes [fact], recommend [bounded action] to advance [Owner outcome], judged by [observable result].** If the basis is uncertain, label it a hypothesis and propose the observation that would settle it.

## v1.7 successor comprehension inside the existing 1b and 2

- **Identity and ownership:** Establish the actual outgoing/incoming responsibility, the predecessor's verified delivery, adjacent peer and delegated workstreams, module interfaces, responsible owner per change and reserved Owner/controller decisions. Actor labels do not prove independent sessions or transfer permissions.
- **Artifact authority matrix:** Independently classify Owner AIM/issue, authentic or protected input, schema/blueprint, candidate or designated golden, benchmark/test receipt and approved product. For each decisive artifact record path/ref, source commit/digest, claimed approval actor and exact purpose *only if witnessed*. Distinguish VERIFIED, REPORTED, CANDIDATE, OWNER_DESIGNATED, OWNER_APPROVED_WITH_SCOPE, UNKNOWN and NOT_RUN. Approval for visual form does not establish academic correctness or rights.
- **Original issue to user gap:** For each Owner outcome explain the actual user/learner behavior delivered today vs still missing. Prior activity, research HTML, QA counts, and promising source drafts cannot substitute for the real end-to-end consumer.
- **Ranked ROI:** At most three bounded alternatives or NO_CHANGE, each with user benefit, observed fact vs hypothesis, one accountable owner, cost/risk, the smallest falsifier, and result-dependent next action.
- These are **investigation obligations, not added stages, signing requirements, mechanized approval or another review loop**.
