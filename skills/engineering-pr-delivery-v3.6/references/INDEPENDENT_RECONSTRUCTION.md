## Applicability

Apply this policy in **both standalone and coordinator modes**. v3.6 uses no
takeover questionnaire, Q1-Q5 exam, agent pass mark, scored admission or compulsory
examiner. Do not import those mechanisms from older delivery protocols, team
templates or predecessor comments. The task self-check and evidence score concern
the delivered work; they do not qualify an incoming agent by examination.
Preserve old question/score artifacts as historical provenance; they are not
active v3.6 admission requirements and must not be rewritten as current authority.

| Current situation | Action |
|---|---|
| BuddyRunner, two-pass or three-pass is selected and in progress | Use only its native stages; add no preliminary or final reconstruction step. |
| That selected flow is completed for this work | Additional reconstruction is NOT_APPLICABLE. Reuse its outputs and continue the next authorized task. No new report or qualification score is required. |
| Cold takeover/recovery without one of those selected flows | Independently reconstruct from authoritative sources using the method below. |
| Continuing the same grounded task | Continue the work. New tasks, commits and replies do not automatically trigger another reconstruction. |

Completion means the selected process was actually performed, not merely that a
prompt was generated. Do not impose a new certification ceremony to establish
that distinction: use the available native outputs/records. If a needed part is
unavailable, retrieve or check that part and state the limit. Material new facts
receive a targeted update; they do not automatically reopen a completed flow.
The next assignment still carries its necessary purpose, inputs, boundaries and
evidence. If its execution exposes a misunderstanding, correct that specific
problem and adjust support without restarting a qualification process.

Keep protocols explicitly selected. An existing repository still selecting another
delivery protocol is not silently migrated by linking this guide; make a visible,
Owner-authorized scoped selection where necessary. Existing ownership, protected
engineering authority, acceptance and merge limits remain actual constraints.
Do not use those limits as a reason to recreate a v3.6 qualification exam.

## Establish the project model

Start with the Owner's original words and later amendments, not the predecessor's
conclusion or a checklist of topics. Read accessible originals and current state.
Preserve useful earlier evidence while treating summaries and reported PASS as
claims until their relevant basis is inspected. No responsive predecessor is needed.

Build a connected account in your own words, sized to the actual problem:

- **Purpose and whole outcome:** why the Owner wants the work, the user journey or
  technical result, what success means and what remains beyond the current task.
- **Actual basis:** exact candidate/base or artifact, authentic inputs and important
  digests, governing decisions, available authority, current ownership and changes.
- **Causal model:** trace a concrete input/action through producers, state changes,
  consumers and the observable result. Explain load-bearing engineering assumptions,
  units, identities or dependencies where they affect the next decision.
- **Established versus unresolved:** distinguish FACT, CLAIM, HYPOTHESIS and UNKNOWN.
  Cite the sources/witnesses that establish decisive statements. Keep historical
  results attached to their actual inputs/candidate and identify competing accounts.
- **Direction:** explain the next useful action, retained/deferred work, meaningful
  failure contrast and high-ROI improvements. Compare alternatives when the choice
  is consequential; do not add redesign merely to appear independent.

These are connected responsibilities, not five questions or mandatory sections.
A short coherent account is sufficient for a small task. Use the existing native
record and link detail rather than imposing a second reporting schema.
Prefer improvements with clear Owner benefit or uncertainty reduction relative to
effort, regression risk and integration/handoff cost. Keep useful discoveries and
deferred work visible without diverting from the unresolved user outcome.
An optional [note template](../templates/reconstruction-v36.md) helps an agent that
needs structure; completing headings does not demonstrate understanding.

Check execution readiness early: actual checkout, input bytes, dependencies, test
runner, application route and browser/tool access relevant to the next task.
An absent binary or runner cannot be repaired by another source-only design essay.
Prefer a feasible execution host or a useful independent subtask when appropriate.

## Challenge the reconstruction

Check a load-bearing explanation against source or actual behavior. Select a
counterexample that could change the continuation, such as stale state, wrong
units, a copied benchmark answer, an uncommitted input or a disconnected consumer.
State expected versus observed evidence and what plausible wrong result remains.
A correct source trace alone does not demonstrate runtime behavior.

Use an independent expected value/route when the claim requires it; do not use the
production helper as its own correctness oracle. Inspect shared upstream premises,
prior reference exposure and evidence applicability. A fresh context or different
model label does not establish a distinct principal or blind review.

Assess the account by whether it explains the real system and supports a sound next
action. If a material misunderstanding appears, correct the affected source/model
and revise that part. Do not demand an exam, marks, fixed rounds, fixed agent count
or repeated restatement of the entire project. A narrower agent may gather evidence;
retain coupled model/integration decisions with a participant able to justify them.

## Continue useful work

Publish one concise reconstruction/continuation note in the existing authorized
record, when a new reconstruction is applicable. Include the exact basis, decisive
sources, unresolved facts and proposed bounded action. Do not publish to a new
channel without authority. The note itself is not implementation.

Continue actions already authorized once the source model is usable. Ask the Owner
only about consequential missing intent or authority; first resolve discoverable
facts from the repository. Present a concrete scope/branch/custody decision if one
is truly required. Do not invent a blanket HOLD because a reconstruction score or
another role is absent.

For a capability restriction, retain the attempted action, observed restriction,
affected scope and next feasible action. Preserve completed evidence and continue
unaffected work. Product validation, independent review where actually required,
and Owner acceptance retain their meaning; reconstruction does not substitute for
any of them. Existing native evidence, A13/A14 and terminal LOG remain unchanged.

## Use the available participants

In standalone mode, the executing agent reconstructs and the Owner receives the
continuation where needed; another Oracle/examiner is not required. In coordinator
mode, the available lead reconciles relevant findings and retains integrated work.
Neither arrangement adds a scored qualification gate. Existing team roles do not
change from this policy; their actual review/custody powers remain separately bound.

Independent reconstruction means reasoning from original sources rather than
reciting the outgoing account. Disclose self-review/exposure. A source-based
reconstruction can be useful without qualifying as an independent product review.

## Examples

**Cold UI recovery:** a predecessor says a working button proves the workflow is
fixed. The incoming agent reads the original pointer-interaction requirement,
actual route/handlers and known trace; it distinguishes a programmatic click from
ordinary pointer behavior, checks access to the actual failing scenario, and
chooses a discriminating observation before proposing a repair. If the browser is
unavailable, it states that restriction and the executable next route rather than
claiming UI PASS or issuing an exam.

**Completed BuddyRunner / two-pass / three-pass:** the relevant native outputs are
available and the next task is already authorized. Reuse them, read the code/input
needed for that task and execute it. Do not issue a separate reconstruction note,
run an extra pass, score the successor or repeat the completed process. If the
candidate changes, inspect the affected change as ordinary work and retain valid
evidence; a commit alone does not reset the context process.

The [constructed application receipt](../tests/evidence/reconstruction-2026-10-19/RECEIPT.md)
preserves an actual fresh use on four local records, its execution witnesses and
limits. It is not evidence that the live LFEA pilot or a full context flow ran.
